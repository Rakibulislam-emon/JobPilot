JobDesk Capture

Connect the extension to your tracker.

Local dev: http://localhost:3000 — deployed: your Vercel URL.
Find it on your JobDesk Settings page (EXTENSION_TOKEN).

How to capture

  1. Open a job post in any tab.
  2. Press Alt+Shift+J or click the toolbar icon.
  3. Review the extracted fields and hit Save.
  4. For Facebook image posts, the screenshot is read by AI.
